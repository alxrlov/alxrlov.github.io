Privacy Policy

Material Merge Block Puzzle is an offline number puzzle. This policy describes the version prepared on 12 November 2026.

Game data

The game stores your board, score, best score, sound preference and language choice locally on your device. This version does not send that information to a developer server, requires no account and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Support

If you email alxrlov@gmail.com, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Support correspondence stays in the support mailbox until it is deleted. You may request deletion using the same address. Email is handled by the email service providers used by you and the developer.

Control and deletion

You can remove local game data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Local progress is not a cloud backup and may be lost when device data is removed.

Platform services

Google Play and Android may process information independently under their own policies. The developer does not control that processing.

Updates and contact

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. For privacy questions, contact the developer of Material Merge Block Puzzle at alxrlov@gmail.com.
