ApexMoon Privacy Policy

Updated November 15, 2026

This policy applies to ApexMoon: Moon Tracker, an Android app by Apex Interactive Studio. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics.

Location and on-device storage

You can choose a city manually or ask ApexMoon to use your device location. Android lets you grant approximate or precise location. ApexMoon requests device location only when you use the location button, and uses the selected location to calculate local lunar times on your device.

The selected place, its coordinates and time zone, your language preference and scheduled reminders are stored in the app’s private storage. Reminders are scheduled on your device using Android and do not transmit location.

Optional weather and city search

When you request a weather forecast, ApexMoon sends the selected place’s latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. This can include your device’s approximate or precise coordinates if you have selected your current location. When you choose worldwide city search, the app sends your search text to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service over HTTPS. Both requests disclose your network IP address to the provider. The lunar calendar, lunar calculations and offline city search work without these requests.

OpenMeteo GmbH handles those requests under its own terms. Its free API policy says its logs may contain IP addresses and coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. See the Open-Meteo privacy notice.

Choices and deletion

You can select a city manually, skip online lookups, disable notifications or revoke location permission in Android Settings. Clear ApexMoon’s app storage or uninstall it to remove its locally saved data. For a request about Open-Meteo’s server logs, contact the provider using the details in its privacy notice.

For privacy questions, email alxrlov@gmail.com. If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond to your request.
