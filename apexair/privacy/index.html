ApexAir Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers ApexAir: Air Quality Forecast, Android package com.apexinteractivestudio.apexair. The developer can be contacted at alxrlov@gmail.com.

What stays on your device
Your saved places, selected locations, language, appearance, AQI scale, alert preferences and cached forecasts are stored in the app’s private storage. Map tiles may also be cached locally. There is no ApexAir account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend in this version.

Forecast and search requests
ApexAir sends the coordinates of the selected place to Open-Meteo to retrieve air-quality data. City searches send the text you enter, such as a city name or postal code, and the requested language to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service. Like other internet services, these providers receive your network IP address and technical request information. Requests use HTTPS.

Optional location
If you press the location button and grant Android’s approximate-location permission, the app uses that location to request the local forecast. You can use city search and saved places without granting location access. The app does not request precise-location or background-location permission, and does not track your movements in the background. A manually selected point on the map is used as a forecast location.

Maps
MapLibre renders the map on your device. Map styles, fonts and vector tiles are requested from OpenFreeMap using OpenStreetMap data. The map service receives the areas you view, request URLs, your IP address and ordinary network information. The app does not create a map of your location history.

Widgets and alerts
If widgets or alerts are enabled, Android may run periodic background checks for the primary saved place. These checks request air-quality data for that saved coordinate; they do not obtain a fresh device location. Notification permission is used only for the air-quality alerts you enable. Checks are periodic and subject to Android’s scheduling and battery restrictions.

Provider retention
Open-Meteo’s published free-API policy states that server logs may include IP addresses and geographic coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. OpenFreeMap’s policy describes anonymized access logs, error logs that may contain IP addresses for up to 7 days, and temporary security logging for up to 30 days. Providers operate under their own terms and may update them. We cannot promise that all provider processing is ephemeral or control their server logs.
Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
OpenFreeMap: https://openfreemap.org/privacy/

Your choices and deletion
You can remove saved places in the app, disable alerts, remove widgets and revoke location or notification permission in Android Settings. Clear ApexAir’s app storage or uninstall the app to remove its local preferences and caches. There is no account to delete. For privacy or deletion questions, contact alxrlov@gmail.com. Requests concerning independent providers’ logs may also need to be directed to those providers. If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to handle your request.

No health profile
The app displays environmental information and general outdoor-activity guidance. It does not collect medical records, symptoms, fitness records or Health Connect data. Model estimates are not local sensor measurements or medical advice. The app is a general-purpose information tool, not a service designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact
If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. Contact: alxrlov@gmail.com.
